# Privacy

This app is unofficial.
It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by HubSpot, Inc.
"HubSpot" is a trademark of HubSpot, Inc.

## What the app does with your data

- The app has no telemetry.
- The app has no analytics.
- The app makes network requests only to the HubSpot hosts you sign in to.
- For update checks, the app also contacts GitHub Releases for this repo.
- Your HubSpot session cookie stays in the local Electron profile on your computer.
- The optional HubSpot private-app token is stored locally and encrypted.
