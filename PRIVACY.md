# Privacy

This app is unofficial.
It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by HubSpot, Inc.
"HubSpot" is a trademark of HubSpot, Inc.

## What the app does with your data

- The app has no telemetry.
- The app has no analytics.
- The app adds no network requests of its own, except update checks against GitHub Releases for this repo.
- The HubSpot web app that runs inside the window makes the same requests that it makes in a browser. HubSpot's own privacy policy covers those.
- Sign-in pages for Google and Microsoft load inside the app when you choose those sign-in methods. Other links open in your default browser.
- Your HubSpot session cookie stays in the local Electron profile on your computer.
- If you add a HubSpot private-app token, the app stores it on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's credential store.
